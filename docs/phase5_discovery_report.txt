4. DOMAIN BOUNDARY DISCOVERY — WHAT "BOOKING"/"SERVICE" MEAN HERE
=======================================================================
BOOKING — what is being booked?
  The repository provides NO definition. The roadmap title is
  "Phase 5 — Booking / Service" and the single clarifying line is
  "Booking belongs to Phase 5" (a scoping statement about the frozen
  rental Reservation, not a domain definition). No document defines
  whether a booking reserves a service, a staff member, a resource
  (room/equipment), a time slot, or a combination. CANDIDATE
  interpretations exist (service-booking, staff-booking,
  resource-booking, appointment = service+staff+slot) but the
  repository cannot arbitrate between them.
  STATUS: UNRESOLVED BUSINESS DECISION (B1).

SERVICE — what is a service?
  No Service model exists. The word "service" in the codebase refers
  only to NestJS services (injectables). Nothing establishes whether
  a service is (a) a sellable catalog item (like Product but
  bookable), (b) a scheduled/performed event, (c) a resource-consuming
  operation, or (d) something else. The existing Product family
  (Product/Variant/Price) is GOODS-oriented (variants+SKUs+stock),
  and nothing says services reuse it.
  STATUS: UNRESOLVED BUSINESS DECISION (B2).

14. CONCURRENCY / INTEGRITY DISCOVERY (hazards identified, NOT solved)
=======================================================================
  Likely Phase 5 hazards and the enforcement layer the REPOSITORY'S
  PRECEDENTS suggest (final choice = decision):
  - Double booking / overlapping appointments on the same target
    (staff or resource or slot): the Reservation EXCLUDE
    constraint is the proven DB-authoritative pattern (btree_gist
    already installed; WHERE-status scoping for terminal states);

  - Concurrent cancel + reschedule + book on the same window:
    guarded state transitions (P4 T2/T3 style) + the exclusion
    constraint's WHERE clause.
  - Capacity races (if group bookings approved): guarded
    conditional updateMany (inventory pattern).
  - Duplicate booking requests (double-click): client-generated
    idempotency key (PosOperation pattern) or natural-key uniques
    — a decision.
  - Price/duration tampering: server-authoritative pricing
    (Price/OrderItem snapshot precedent); duration is UNRESOLVED
    since no duration representation exists.
